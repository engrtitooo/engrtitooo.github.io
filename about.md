      <h2>Experience that shapes how I work.</h2>
      <p>I’m a <strong>Senior Mechanical Engineer and Project Manager</strong> based in Chicago, with more than 18 years of engineering and infrastructure experience. In Metra’s Capital Delivery team, my work includes mechanical design review, HVAC/MEP coordination, and the delivery of facility improvement projects.</p>
      <p>Infrastructure teaches you to look beyond an individual component. A design decision can affect construction, maintenance, cost, and day-to-day operations. I approach technical work by making those relationships explicit and helping teams reach clear, informed decisions.</p>
      <h2>Building beyond the physical system.</h2>
      <p>Alongside my engineering career, I develop software products that address practical user needs. My portfolio includes <strong>WizeCV</strong>, a bilingual AI career platform, and <strong>SpinFund</strong>, a fintech venture focused on cash-flow planning and private savings circles.</p>
      <p>Other projects explore conversational AI, generative design, real-time communication, and learning tools. Together, they represent hands-on experience turning a product idea into requirements, interfaces, integrations, and deployed applications.</p>
      <h2>My approach to AI.</h2>
      <p>I use AI-assisted development tools to explore solutions, generate and refine code, and accelerate iteration. I also integrate AI services into user-facing features, including resume workflows, voice interaction, and image generation.</p>
      <p>My contribution is the direction and review of that work: defining the problem, making product and architecture decisions, validating behavior, and improving the experience. AI is a useful development tool; sound engineering judgment remains essential.</p>
